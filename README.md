## Hotel Booking Trends & Customer Behavior Analytics

## Data Science

### **Python | Pandas | NumPy | EDA | Statistical Analysis | Hospitality Analytics | Business Intelligence**

## Project Overview

Developed a Python-based Hotel Booking Analytics project to investigate reservation patterns, booking cancellations, seasonal demand, customer preferences, and hotel booking performance.

Applied data cleaning, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), descriptive statistics, feature analysis, and data visualization to transform hotel reservation records into meaningful insights relevant to hospitality operations, customer behavior, and booking management.

The project aims to support data-driven decisions related to reservation management, cancellation monitoring, seasonal planning, and customer experience.

## Business Problem

Hotels face challenges in managing reservation cancellations, understanding seasonal demand fluctuations, identifying customer booking preferences, and improving operational planning.

High cancellation rates and changing booking patterns can create uncertainty in room availability and revenue planning.

This project addresses these challenges through exploratory analysis of hotel reservation data, providing a foundation for evidence-based hospitality management decisions.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze hotel booking trends and reservation patterns.
- Investigate booking cancellations and associated factors.
- Examine seasonal demand and monthly reservation activity.
- Analyze customer preferences and booking characteristics.
- Compare booking patterns across hotel types and customer segments.
- Evaluate reservation lead times and length-of-stay patterns.
- Generate business insights relevant to hotel operations and booking management.

## Project Highlights

1. **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:** Prepared hotel booking records using Python, Pandas, and NumPy by examining missing values, duplicate records, data types, and dataset consistency.

2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Investigated booking patterns, hotel types, reservation channels, customer segments, length of stay, and available booking attributes.

3. **Booking Cancellation Analysis:** Examined cancellation trends across customer segments, booking channels, reservation lead times, and booking characteristics.

4. **Seasonal Demand Analysis:** Analyzed monthly and seasonal reservation patterns to understand fluctuations in hotel booking activity.

5. **Customer Behavior Analysis:** Explored guest preferences, repeat bookings, customer segments, and reservation characteristics to understand differences in booking behavior.

6. **Hotel Performance Analysis:** Compared booking activity across hotel categories and relevant customer segments to evaluate reservation patterns.

7. **Statistical & Feature Analysis:** Applied descriptive statistics and exploratory comparisons to investigate relationships between booking attributes, customer characteristics, and reservation outcomes.

8. **Hospitality KPI Analysis:** Evaluated booking-related indicators, including reservation volume, cancellation rate, lead time, and length of stay.

9. **Data Visualization & Reporting:** Used Matplotlib and Seaborn to communicate booking distributions, cancellation patterns, customer segments, and seasonal demand.

10. **Business Insights & Decision Support:** Interpreted reservation trends to identify potential opportunities for cancellation management, demand planning, and customer experience improvement.
