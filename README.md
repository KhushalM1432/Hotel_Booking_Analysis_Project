## Hotel_Booking_Analysis | Data_Science & Hospitality_Analytics_Project | Python

## Data_Science

### Project Highlights

1. **Business Problem Analysis:** Analyzed hotel booking data to understand booking behavior, cancellation trends, seasonal demand, and factors associated with reservation performance.

2. **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:** Used Python and Pandas to inspect data quality, handle missing values, remove duplicate records where appropriate, and prepare structured data for reliable analysis.

3. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Investigated booking patterns, customer characteristics, reservation channels, length of stay, and other available booking attributes to identify meaningful trends.

4. **Booking Cancellation Analysis:** Examined cancellation rates across relevant customer segments, booking channels, lead times, and reservation characteristics to identify patterns associated with cancellations.

5. **Seasonal Demand Analysis:** Analyzed booking activity across available months and seasons to understand demand fluctuations and identify periods of higher or lower reservation activity.

6. **Customer Behavior Analysis:** Explored customer preferences, repeat-guest patterns, market segments, and booking characteristics to understand differences in reservation behavior.

7. **Statistical & Feature Analysis:** Applied descriptive statistics and examined relationships between booking attributes and outcomes to identify factors associated with successful bookings and cancellations.

8. **Data Visualization & Interpretation:** Used Matplotlib and Seaborn to create charts that communicate booking trends, cancellation patterns, customer segments, and seasonal variations.

9. **Hospitality Business Insights:** Translated analytical findings into observations relevant to reservation management, demand planning, cancellation monitoring, and customer experience improvement.

10. **Revenue & Performance Analysis:** Where the required fields were available, examined booking revenue, average daily rates (ADR), and differences in booking value across customer segments or seasons.

11. **Data-Driven Recommendations:** Developed evidence-based recommendations from the analysis to help hotel stakeholders evaluate booking policies, monitor cancellation risks, and improve reservation management strategies.

12. **Analytical Problem-Solving:** Applied a structured data science workflow, from data preparation and exploratory analysis to visualization and interpretation, to investigate practical hospitality business questions.

13. **Reproducible Analytical Workflow:** Organized the analysis in Jupyter Notebook and documented the required Python libraries and setup instructions to support review and reproducibility.

## Tools & Technical Skills

Python, Jupyter Notebook, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Data Cleaning, Data Preprocessing, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), Descriptive Statistics, Feature Analysis, Data Visualization, Booking Analytics, Cancellation Analysis, and Business Insight Generation.

## Project Objective

To transform hotel booking records into actionable insights by analyzing reservation patterns, cancellation behavior, customer preferences, and seasonal demand, supporting data-driven decisions in hospitality operations and booking management.
